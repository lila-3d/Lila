# Rigid Bind

Scales on a curling tail and armour lames on a bending elbow, each piece kept rigid by a Rigid Bind deformer: they ride the skin, stack over each other where it squeezes and fan out where it stretches.

## Use it

1. Open `RigidBind.lila` and press play (120 frames at 30 fps).
2. Look through `Cam_Tail`, `Cam_Arm` or `Cam_All`: select one and use the view's Panels > Look Through Selected.
3. Select `ScalesBind` or `LamesBind` (in the Scales or Lames mesh's stack) and change its settings while scrubbing: Collision Shape `off` lets pieces pass through each other, Collision Lift `0` makes them slide apart instead of stacking.

| Setup | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Scales | 88 overlapping plates on a tail skinned to five joints; the upper rows climb over the lower ones as it curls |
| Lames | Nine part rings on the outside of an elbow; relaxation keeps them evenly fanned as the arm bends to 90 degrees |

To send either to Unreal, see the Rigid Bind page in the docs: `rigidBindToSkin("Scales")` turns the scales into skin weights on the tail's joints.

## Troubleshooting

- Nothing moves: the joints are keyed; check the time slider is inside frames 1 to 120.
- `RigidBind.lila` is generated by `build_rigid_bind.py`; rerun it (`lilabatch --batch build_rigid_bind.py`) to rebuild the scene.

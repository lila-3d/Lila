# Destruction

Four concrete walls blown apart by seven explosions, then the slab they stand on rumbles and gives way from one side to the other, dropping everything into the pit below. The ground around the pit stays put. The same shot is built two ways:

- `Destruction_Tools.lila`: menu commands only, no node editing.
- `Destruction_Nodes.lila`: one hand-written node group, `CourtyardCollapse`, with the walls made inside it.

## Use it

1. Open either scene and press play (240 frames at 30 fps). The first pass simulates as it plays; scrubbing back is instant after that.
2. Look through `ShotCam` for the framed shot.
3. Send it to Unreal: select `Destruction` (tools scene) or `Ground` (nodes scene), then **Effects > Destruction > Send to Unreal as Vertex Animation Texture**.

How the tools scene is made, step by step. Break Apart and Add Explosion are also in the Effects > Destruction menu:

```python
ops.breakApart("Ground", "WallA", "WallB", "WallC", "WallD", colliders=["RimNorth", "RimSouth", "RimWest", "RimEast"])
ops.addExplosion("Destruction", position=[-1500, 110, -640], frame=20, blast=1500, radius=300)
ops.collapse("Destruction", part="Ground", start=125, duration=75, direction=[1, 0, 0.25], rumble=4, depth=2500)
```

## Troubleshooting

- A wall sags before it's hit: raise its pieces' strength (`strength` on the modifier), or lower the pieces count.
- Debris flies off the slab: lower that explosion's blast (`Explosion` nodes in the group, `speed`) or move it closer to the wall.
- The ground falls too evenly or too raggedly: `raggedness` on the Collapse node, 0 for a straight front.
- Both `.lila` files are generated by `build_destruction_tools.py` and `build_destruction_nodes.py`; rerun them to rebuild.

## Tune it

- Tools scene: the `Destruction_BreakModifier` shows seed, roughness, strength, start frame and each mesh's piece count. Explosions and the collapse are nodes in its group (open it in the Node Editor), or run `addExplosion` and `collapse` again.
- Nodes scene: the modifier shows Ground Pieces, Wall Pieces, Seed, Strength, Blast (scales every explosion), Collapse Start, Collapse Duration and Rumble.
- Share the nodes version by giving someone `CourtyardCollapse.lilanodes` (Node Editor > Import) and applying it to any slab.

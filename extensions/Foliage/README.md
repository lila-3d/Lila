# Foliage

Plant modelling, as two tool sets:

- [Tree Generator](TreeGenerator.md): **Create > Tree Generator**. Trunks, branches, canopy and leaves, exported as a bake-ready high and low poly pair.
- [Foliage Blades](Blades.md): **Mesh > Foliage Blades**. Grows blades from the faces of a modelled shrub and varies each one's extrude and deformers.
